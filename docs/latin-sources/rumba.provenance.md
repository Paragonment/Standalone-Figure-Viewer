# Rumba dataset - provenance

Output: `rumba.csv` - 41 figures, 225 step rows. Bronze 27 figures, Silver 7, Gold 7.
Generated by `scripts/gen_rumba.py` and checked by `scripts/validate.py`: 19 fields per row, steps run consecutively from 1, Level is always Bronze/Silver/Gold, figure-level fields appear only on step 1, every figure's beats add up to whole bars, and there are no em dashes.

## Sources used

1. **ISTD, Latin American Professional Modular Examinations Syllabus (June 2018)** - official list of Rumba figures by level: Associate 1-16, Licentiate adds 17-21, Fellowship adds 22-26.
   https://www.istd.org/documents/latin-american-professional-modular-examinations-syllabus-2018/latin-modular-syllabus-june-18-complete-.pdf
   Level mapping used: figures 1-16 = Bronze, 17-21 = Silver, 22-26 = Gold. This is the Associate/Licentiate/Fellow mapping given in SCHEMA.md.
2. **British Dance Council, Rules Appendix 1 (Latin)** - restricted syllabus list. It names "The Laird Technique of Latin Dancing" as its basis, and it cross-checks figure names and variants (e.g. Alemana from Fan / Open Position with L to R or R to R hold, Spiral to Fan / Open / Open CPP, Advanced Opening Out Movement).
   https://www.britishdancecouncil.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/RulesAppendix1Latin.pdf
3. **Dance Central (dancecentral.info), International Rumba figure pages** - step-by-step Leader/Follower charts with timing (2, 3, 4 1), feet positions, amount of turn, alternative endings, and precedes/follows. These closely follow current ISTD/Laird wording ("Cuban Cross", "Contact Position", "body turns less", "1/8 to L over 2-3"). This was the primary source for the step charts.
   https://www.dancecentral.info/ballroom/international-style/rumba (plus one page per figure, e.g. /rumba/hockey-stick, /rumba/three-threes, /rumba/circular-hip-twists)
4. **IDTA Theory - RUMBA flashcards ("Laird's Technique of Latin Dancing - Rumba")** - Laird-derived, but from an older Laird/IDTA edition. Per-step Man/Lady foot positions, footwork, actions and body turn for: Basic Movement, Fan, Hockey Stick, Spot Turns, Underarm Turns, Hand to Hand, Natural Top, Closed Hip Twist, Alemana, Open Hip Twist, Reverse Top, Opening Out to R and L, Advanced Opening Out Movement, Spiral and Curl. Also footwork principles: ball flat throughout, flat on cucaracha weight transfers, toe then flat on delayed walks and Cuban crosses.
   https://freezingblue.com/flashcards/341477/preview/idta-theory-rumba
5. **Roundalab Rumba Standards, Phases IV-VI (round-dance adaptation of International figures)** - independent Man/Woman descriptions used to cross-check: Alemana, Hockey Stick, Fan, Closed/Open/Advanced/Continuous/Circular Hip Twist, Natural Top, Natural Opening Out, Full Reverse Top, Curl, Rope Spin, Three Alemanas, Three Threes, Alternative Basic and Aida.
   https://roundalab.org/wp-content/uploads/Standards/4-RB.pdf , .../5-RB.pdf , .../6-RB.pdf
6. Checked but not usable: copies of Laird's book on Scribd and pdfcoffee (text is not exposed, and pdfcoffee downloads are behind a captcha); the German Wikibooks Rumba draft (no usable charts); geocities and superdancing pages (403 errors and connection resets).

## Verification against Laird or Laird-derived sources

Verified against the IDTA Laird flashcards (4) and Dance Central (3):
- Closed Basic, Open Basic (DC note plus IDTA), Fan, Hockey Stick, Spot Turn to L/R, Underarm Turn to L/R, Hand to Hand to R/L Side Position, Natural Top, Closed Hip Twist, Alemana, Open Hip Twist, Opening Out to R and L, Spiral, Curl.

Verified against Dance Central plus Roundalab:
- Natural Opening Out Movement, Advanced Hip Twist, Continuous Hip Twist, Circular Hip Twist (Man), Three Alemanas, Three Threes, Rope Spinning, Reverse Top, Alternative Basic, Aida.

From Dance Central only, consistent with the ISTD list and BDC names:
- Basic in Place, Cucarachas, New York to L/R Side Position, Shoulder to Shoulder L/R Side, Progressive Walks Forward/Back, Side Steps to L/R, Cuban Rocks, Opening Out from Reverse Top, Sliding Doors, Fencing.

Footwork comes from the IDTA Laird principles: BF by default; F on cucaracha-type weight transfers (except forward cucarachas) and on Cuban Rocks; "T then F" on Cuban Cross, delayed-walk and pressed-walk steps; "BF (T of LF)" and similar on the Lady's spiral, curl and spin steps, as Dance Central describes them. Laird's Rumba charts mostly give footwork as a general "ball flat" rule rather than per step, so the per-step values here are an application of that rule.

## Modelling decisions

- Named variants are separate figures: Closed / Open / In Place / Alternative Basic; New York to L / R Side Position; Spot Turn to L / R; Underarm Turn to L / R; Shoulder to Shoulder L / R Side; Hand to Hand to R / L Side Position; Progressive Walks Forward / Back; Side Steps to L / R.
- Cucarachas are one 6-step figure (LF then RF Cucaracha). Cuban Rocks are the 3-step LF Cuban Rock. RF and syncopated versions are described in the notes.
- Switch Turns are covered in the Spot Turn notes, since they use the same steps with a hand hold.
- Spiral Turns (ISTD figure 21) are split into Spiral, Curl and Rope Spinning. The three Hip Twists (figure 26) are split into Advanced, Continuous and Circular.
- Not included: older Laird/IDTA figures that are not in the current ISTD list, i.e. Check from Open CPP / Open PP (now New York), Advanced Opening Out Movement (mentioned in the Closed Hip Twist notes) and Fallaway.
- Alignment columns are empty, as the schema asks for Rumba spot figures.

## Uncertainties (please review)

- **Hockey Stick, Man step 4:** Dance Central (current) has "RF behind LF (Cuban Cross), 1/8 to R"; the older IDTA/Laird edition has "RF back". The current version is used.
- **Hockey Stick, Lady 4-6 turn:** Dance Central has 1/8 L, then 1/2 L (body turns less), then body completes. The older IDTA edition has 1/8, 3/8, 1/8. Dance Central is used.
- **Fan, Lady turn:** the chart is 1/8 L on 5 and 1/8 L on 6, but Dance Central notes say she may turn 3/8 L over 5-6; IDTA gives 3/8 then 1/4. The chart values are used, and the alternative is in the notes.
- **Alemana, Lady:** the current version (Dance Central) has three forward walks turning 1 1/8 R over 4-6. The older IDTA version has step 4 as a delayed walk with LF across without weight (outside edge of toe, then flat). The current version is used.
- **Natural Top amount of turn:** "up to 2 complete turns over 1-8" (Dance Central). IDTA says 1/4 per step, 2 1/4 in total.
- **Reverse Top:** the current 9-step version starts with Man RF side in Contact Position. The older IDTA version has 6 steps, starting LF crossed in front. The 9-step version is used.
- **Opening Out from Reverse Top:** charted as 3 steps after Reverse Top 1-6 (Dance Central). Laird may present it differently, possibly with the Reverse Top steps included in the count.
- **Shoulder to Shoulder:** the amount of turn is adapted from Dance Central's Cha Cha-numbered "1/4 to L over 3-5 (feet only)". It is shown as "Feet turn 1/4 to L (R), body turns less" on step 3.
- **Circular Hip Twist, Lady steps 5, 7 and 9:** Dance Central says "Close RF to LF, 1/2 to L" (pressed walk), and that is what is used. Roundalab describes them as "swivel 1/2 L, step fwd R". The Man's part matches Dance Central (side / Cuban Cross alternating, 1/8 L per step, followed by 4-6 Advanced Hip Twist). Roundalab ends with a close instead.
- **Sliding Doors:** the position names (R Shadow Position at 6 and 12, Tandem Position at 9 and 11) are read from Dance Central's "RSP" and "TP" abbreviations.
- **Aida:** charted as the 3 back steps only. The endings (Cuban Rock and Spot Turn, Double Spot Turn, side Cucaracha) are in the notes. Starting position is given as Contact Position (after 1-6 Reverse Top), though it can also be started from Open Position.
- **Lady's feet positions on spot and underarm turns:** written in the current "fwd and across in line ..., then turn to end ... back" form. IDTA phrases the same steps as "fwd across body turning to finish back and slightly to side".
- No primary text of the Laird 7th/8th edition could be read directly. Everything that says "Laird" relies on Laird-derived secondary sources (sources 3 and 4).
