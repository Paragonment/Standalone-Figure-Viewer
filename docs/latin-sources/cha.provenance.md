# Cha Cha Cha dataset - provenance

Output: `cha.csv` (49 figures, 508 step rows). Generated by `scripts/cha/build_cha.py`, validated by `scripts/cha/validate.py` (19 fields per row, header exact, steps consecutive from 1, single valid level per figure, figure-level fields only on step 1, no em/en dashes, Lead/Follow foot alternation check per weight change, beat totals printed per figure).

## Levels

Levels follow the current ISTD Cha Cha Cha syllabus (sources 4-6), which differs from Laird/IDTA's own A/M/F markers (source 2). Notable differences: ISTD has Closed Hip Twist at Bronze (Laird: Member/Silver); Open Hip Twist, Cross Basic and Cuban Breaks at Silver (Laird: Cross Basic Silver, Cuban Breaks Fellow/Gold); Turkish Towel, Sweetheart, Follow My Leader, Advanced Hip Twist, Hip Twist Spiral, Foot Changes at Gold. Laird-only developments (Open Hip Twist Spiral, Syncopated Open Hip Twist, Continuous Circular Hip Twist) are placed at Gold.

## Figures verified against Laird (sources 1 and/or 2)

Bronze: Closed Basic, Open Basic, Time Step, New York to L Side Position (Laird "Check from Open CPP"), New York to R Side Position ("Check from Open PP"), Spot Turn to L, Spot Turn to R, Underarm Turn to R, Underarm Turn to L, Shoulder to Shoulder, Hand to Hand, Fan, Alemana, Hockey Stick (1 + 2 + 3), Natural Top (1 + 2 + 3), Closed Hip Twist (1 + 2 + 3).
Silver: Open Hip Twist (1 + 2 + 3), Curl (1 + 3), Cross Basic (1 + 2 + 3), Cuban Break in Open Position (1 + 2), Cuban Break in Open CPP (1 + 2), Split Cuban Break in Open CPP (1 + 2), Split Cuban Break from Open CPP and Open PP (1 + 2).
Gold: Hip Twist Spiral (Laird "Close Hip Twist Spiral", 1 + 2), Open Hip Twist Spiral (1 + 2), Syncopated Open Hip Twist (1, prose description only), Continuous Circular Hip Twist (1), Turkish Towel (1 + 2 + 3), Sweetheart (1 + 3), Follow My Leader (1 + 3), Foot Changes Methods 1-3 (1 + 2 + 3).
Three Cha Cha Chas Forward/Back are built from Laird's lock-step charts and his Open Basic note (two extra locks), corroborated by 3.

## Figures from ISTD-derived source only (3), not in Laird

Basic in Place, Switch Turn to L, Switch Turn to R, Side Step to L, Side Step to R, There and Back, Natural Opening Out Movement, Reverse Top, Opening Out from Reverse Top, Aida, Spiral, Rope Spinning, Chase, Advanced Hip Twist. Footwork for these is not given by the source; Laird's general conventions were applied (BF on normal steps, T on the step crossing behind in a forward lock and on the first step of a backward lock, B for swivelling/ball-only steps).

## Uncertainties and editorial decisions

- Several dancebooks.ru rows contain transcription slips that were corrected by foot-alternation logic and source 2: Closed Hip Twist Lady steps 6-7 (feet columns swapped), Time Step steps 5/10 (wrong foot named), Natural Top ending A step 15, Cross Basic Lady steps 4-5, Curl Lady step 4, Split Cuban Break in Open CPP step 5, Hand to Hand Lady step 13, Open Basic Lady step 8 footwork (BF in the transcription, T used per Laird's lock chart).
- Hockey Stick man step 6: Laird (both transcriptions) RF back, 1/8 to R; Dance Central gives "RF behind LF (Cuban Cross)". Laird used. Lady step 10: 2014 ed. "LF back", earlier ed. "LF to side".
- Closed Hip Twist / Hip Twist Spiral: Laird man step 1 LF fwd turning 3/8 R with a Ronde Chasse and Lady 3/4 R; ISTD (Dance Central) has man LF to side or fwd in R Side Position with compact chasse and Lady 1/2 R. Laird used; ISTD noted. Man's 8-10: 2014 ed. Hip Twist Chasse (alternative plain chasse turning 1/8 L, which is what the earlier edition charts).
- Shoulder to Shoulder: Laird gives 1/4 L over 3-5 but 1/8 R over 8-10 (Dance Central: 1/4 both); Laird kept.
- Natural Top: Laird gives up to 2 3/4 turns (ISTD up to 3). Man's footwork on the crossed-behind steps ("T, heel lowers as next step taken") is an interpretation of the transcription's footnote.
- Time Step (Laird) starts with an introductory step on count 1, so it totals 9 beats; steps 2-11 are the two-bar unit. ISTD's Time Step uses a Cuban Cross behind on count 2 instead of a close.
- Follow My Leader (Laird man 28 steps, Lady 26) and Foot Changes Method 3 (man starts on '&' of the preceding beat): rows are aligned to the music with empty Follow cells where only the man steps; Timing/Beat Value are the Lead's. Foot Changes Method 1: man's forward walk turn takes a full beat while Lady dances a back lock, so her 4& is combined in one cell on row 3. Cuban Break in Open Position: partners dance the Cuban Break in alternate bars; the Follow's own counts are given in her Action column.
- Syncopated Open Hip Twist is derived from Laird's prose (steps 6-7 omitted, hold on 2-3, so step 5 has beat value 3); turn amounts copied as charted (Lady "1/2 to R over 7-8").
- Alemana and Natural Top: only ending A is charted step by step; ending B (and ISTD/Laird endings to Open CPP, R-to-R hold Alemana) are described in General Notes. Aida endings, Hockey Stick to Open CPP/L Side Position, Spiral/Curl/Rope Spinning alternative endings are likewise in notes.
- Chasse alternatives (Compact, Ronde, Hip Twist, Slip Close, Runaway, Split Cuban Break chasse, forward/backward runs, locks) are not separate figures; they are used inside figures where Laird charts them and mentioned in notes.
- Opening Out from Reverse Top Lady steps 3-5 are charted as a back lock (source says "LF back, toe turned out, to chasse LRL"); Reverse Top crossing-step footwork for the Lady is BF by convention (not given by source).
- Alignments are not given by Laird for Cha Cha Cha spot figures, so all alignment columns are empty.
