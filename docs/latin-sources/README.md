# Latin figure sources

`public/latin.csv` holds the International Latin figures (ISTD syllabus, Bronze/Silver/Gold)
for Cha Cha Cha, Samba, Rumba, Paso Doble and Jive: 208 figures, 1679 steps.

No readable copy of Walter Laird's "The Laird Technique of Latin Dancing" could be reached
online, so each chart was built from Laird-derived sources and cross-checked between them.
The per-dance files here list the sources, which figures were corroborated, and the open
uncertainties worth checking against the book:

- [Cha Cha Cha](cha.provenance.md)
- [Samba](samba.provenance.md)
- [Rumba](rumba.provenance.md)
- [Paso Doble](paso.provenance.md)
- [Jive](jive.provenance.md)

Also unresolved: in the Paso Doble Fregolina and Farol, the Lead's step 8 (LF in place) is
followed by LF back on step 23 with no weight change in between.

## Columns

Timing and Beat Value are per step and shared by both partners. Feet Positions, Alignment,
Amount of Turn, Footwork and Action are per role (Lead/Follow). Starting Position,
Ending Position and General Notes are figure-level and set on step 1 only.
