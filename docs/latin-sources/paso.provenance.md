# Paso Doble dataset - provenance

Output: `paso.csv` (35 figures, 331 step rows). Generated by `scripts/gen_paso.py` from `scripts/paso_data.py`. Validated with `scripts/paso_validate.py` (19 fields per row, exact header, steps consecutive from 1, Level in Bronze/Silver/Gold, figure-level fields only on step 1, beat values parse, no em dash, no blank lines).

## Sources

1. **Syllabus list (ISTD, matches Laird's figure numbering 1-30)**
   - Dance Central, ISTD Paso Doble syllabus (Newcomer/Bronze/Silver/Gold): https://www.dancecentral.info/ballroom/international-style/paso-doble
   - BallroomDancers.com Paso Doble figure index (Laird numbering 1-30, step charts behind a login so not usable): https://www.ballroomdancers.com/dances/info.asp?sid=573
   - Emotions DanceSport syllabus index: http://www.emotionsdancesport.com/paso-doble.htm
   - ISTD Newcomer figures are put under `Bronze`, as the schema allows only Bronze/Silver/Gold.
2. **Laird-derived step charts (current ISTD / Laird 7th-8th edition wording)**
   - Emotions DanceSport per-figure charts (Foot Position / Turn / Footwork, with Laird-style notes, precedes/follows and alternatives): http://www.emotionsdancesport.com/paso-doble/<figure>.htm (28 figure pages downloaded; the La Passe page is empty and the Grand Circle page wrongly repeats the Huit chart).
   - BallroomGuide (GitHub repo BallroomGuide/BallroomGuide, `latin/paso/*.md`, also https://ballroomguide.github.io/BallroomGuide/latin/paso.html): full Man/Lady charts with alignment, turn, footwork, timing and notes for all 30 figures plus endings.
3. **Older Laird technique (Russian translation, A/L/F levels)**
   - zhestkov.net "Техника исполнения Пасодобль": https://zhestkov.net/Paso_doble . A translation of an older edition of Laird's Paso chapter: Basic Movement, Sur Place, Chasses, Elevations, Huit, Attack, Separation, Sixteen, Twist Turn (old version), Promenade, PP to CPP, Promenade Close, Grand Circle, La Passe, Banderillas, Syncopated Separation, Fallaway Whisk, Spanish Line, Twists, Chasse Cape (old version), Travelling Spins, Flamenco Taps, and the methods of changing feet (Syncopated Chasse, Left Foot Variation, the Coup de Pique variants).
4. **Secondary corroboration (not Laird)**
   - Kandykane Dance notes, figures 1-10: https://ck-kandykane.blogspot.com/2014/07/paso-doble-syllabus-figures-1-10.html
   - Round dance descriptions (G. Langer PDF https://sunburst.lima-city.de/html/download/PasoDoble.pdf ; Sears, Fregolina and Farol: https://www.rounddancing.net/dance/articles/onefigure/pdfregolina.html). Used only for general structure, such as the measures of the Fregolina/Farol and the Farol omitting the Lady's extra passes.
   - Oxford University DanceSport syllabus list: https://www.oudancesport.co.uk/Dances/Paso_Doble (level placement only).
   - German Wikibooks Paso Doble page (figure names and literature): https://de.wikibooks.org/wiki/Tanzen:_Paso_Doble

No scan or PDF of the actual Laird book was found online (searches of archive.org, scribd, pdfcoffee, dokumen.pub and similar sites turned up only spam pages or nothing). The ResearchGate paper "Paso Doble dance technique - a new approach" returned HTTP 403. idans.nl (a mirror of BallroomGuide) was down (HTTP 522).

## Conventions and decisions

- Timing uses the book's 1-8 counting. The second bar of a 16-count figure restarts at 1, as in Laird.
- One row per chart step. Where the Man holds position while the Lady steps (Grand Circle 2-4, La Passe, Fregolina/Farol, Caping Walks), a row is kept for each count with "Hold position (no weight change)", so that the Lead and Follow columns stay aligned by count. Points without weight (Coup de Pique 1, Left Foot Variation, Syncopated Separation, Flamenco Taps taps) are kept as chart steps.
- Drag step 2 (the slow draw over counts 2-3) is kept as its own chart step with timing `2-3` and beat value 2, following the Emotions chart. BallroomGuide charts the Drag as 2 steps (123, 4).
- Alignments: the charted alignment from the sources is used where it is self-consistent. Where a source alignment contradicted its own turn amounts, the alignment was recomputed from the turn amounts, with LOD counter-clockwise and wall on the Man's right. Examples: Open Telemark step 1, Attack turn direction (to L, not R), Grand Circle, Huit Lady step 8 (1/8 to L, not 1/4), Lady's Open Telemark steps 4-6, Promenade Link Lady's turn.
- Ditto marks in the Emotions tables refer to the line above, which belongs to the other partner. They were resolved that way.
- Variants listed by the ISTD as "(also ...)" are separate figures: Deplacement / Attack, Promenade Link / Promenade Close, Fregolina / Farol, Chasse Cape / Outside Turn. Elevations to Right/Left are described in the notes of the Chasses figures. The Basic Movement backward and the Flamenco Taps Method 2 are described in the notes.

## Uncertainties

- **Levels.** The current ISTD list (Dance Central, Emotions) was followed. Older lists put the Attack and Huit at Associate/Bronze, and some put Grand Circle at Silver and Fallaway Whisk/Promenade Close/Spanish Line at Silver. BallroomGuide puts the Attack in Bronze rather than Newcomer, and lists a separate "Syncopated Chasse to Right" (Silver). That figure was not included as its own figure; it is described as Ending 2 of the Syncopated Separation.
- **Promenade Close.** Charted as 2 steps (steps 3-4 of the Promenade Link begun in PP, as used inside the Grand Circle and Travelling Spins from CPP). The older Laird chart has 4 steps (plus RF side, LF close).
- **Promenade Link.** The standard turn used is Man 1/8 to L on 3-4 (ending facing LOD), with the Lady's 3/8 to L derived. BallroomGuide uses the alternative (Man 1/8 to R to face wall, Lady 1/8 to L).
- **Separation family alignment.** These figures start with the Man facing LOD, chosen so that the Separation, Caping Walks and Fallaway Ending are consistent with each other. BallroomGuide starts them facing wall; the old Laird chart starts facing against LOD.
- **Fallaway Ending to Separation.** BallroomGuide charts a different sequence (a side, cross-behind and fallaway turning pattern counted from 8). The Emotions version (both partners walk fwd outside partner turning R, then fallaway, then 2 chassés) was used.
- **Coup de Pique.** The 9-step Emotions chart was used. BallroomGuide gives 3 shorter "methods" (4-6 steps, timing 1 2 3 & 4). The older Laird book has LF-to-RF, RF-to-LF, Couplet and Syncopated versions. Footwork on the fallaway back steps is BF (BallroomGuide, old Laird), whereas Emotions prints "Balls of both feet".
- **Promenade and Counter Promenade footwork.** Man step 5 (side in CPP) is HF per Emotions and BF per BallroomGuide. The standard (U-shaped) amount of turn is charted; practical versions turn more.
- **Grand Circle.** The Emotions page is broken. The chart is from BallroomGuide plus old Laird. Old Laird prints "3/8 to R" for the twist, which is taken as a misprint for 3/8 to L. Commencing alignment (PP moving against LOD) is chosen to match the Huit/Promenade and Counter Promenade conventions.
- **Twists.** The Lady's turn between 8-9 is 1/4 (BallroomGuide); Emotions says 1/8, which does not complete the rotation. Old Laird gives different per-step amounts.
- **Twist Turn and Chasse Cape.** These are the current versions. The older Laird versions differ: the Twist Turn closed fwd, and the Chasse Cape had 24 steps ending in a Press Line.
- **Fregolina/Farol.** These come from a single detailed chart (BallroomGuide) plus round-dance structure. Some Lady alignments on 16-20 are given as movement directions only. The Farol is derived as Fregolina 1-12 + 21-28, as described.
- **Outside Turn.** Only a development note was found: "steps 1-3 of the Chasse Cape then step to side in PP along LOD". The Lady's step 4 (RF to side in PP, facing DC) and the Man's 1/8 to R are inferred.
- **Footwork summaries.** Where a source gives a choice ("B or BF", "HF or BF") both are kept.
